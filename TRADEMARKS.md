# Name, logo and listing assets

The name "Credit Guard", the Credit Guard logo and icon (including the files
in `listing/icon/`, `site/icon-*.png` and `public/favicon.ico`,
`public/apple-touch-icon.png`), and the App Store listing screenshots in

https://apps.shopify.com/credit-guard-b2b-net-terms. All rights in them are
reserved. If you build on this code, publish it under your own name and
artwork, and use your own Shopify app credentials.
