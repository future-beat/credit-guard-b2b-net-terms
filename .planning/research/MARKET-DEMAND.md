## 1. Addressable pool: how many Shopify merchants sell B2B?

### Shopify's own B2B statements (primary sources)

| Period | B2B GMV YoY | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 2025 | **+109%** | https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/05/09/shopify-b2b-ecommerce-sales-q1-fy25/ |
| Q4 2025 | **+84%**; FY2025 **+96%** ("GMV from B2B merchants was up 84% in Q4 and 96% in 2025" — Finkelstein) | https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/SHOP/pressreleases/165570/shopify-shop-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/ |
| Q4 2025 release, 11 Feb 2026 | **"B2B represents a very small portion of total GMV today, given it is a product offering for Plus merchants only."** | https://www.shopify.com/news/shopify-q4-2025-financial-results |
| Q1 2026 (call 5 May 2026) | **+80%**; "we made several other features of our B2B offering available to most of our standard subscription plans" | https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/SHOP/pressreleases/1716522/shopify-shop-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/ |
| Q2 2026 (call 5 Aug 2026) | **+76%**; "we expanded native B2B capabilities beyond Plus for the first time" | https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/SHOP/pressreleases/3809161/shopify-shop-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ |

Context: total GMV $100.7B in Q1 2026 and $115.6B in Q2 2026; FY2025 GMV $378.4B. Shopify does **not** disclose B2B GMV in dollars or a count of B2B merchants anywhere found (10-K, four press releases, three call transcripts, the B2B-for-all announcement). Growth is decelerating (109% → 96% → 84% → 80% → 76%) even after the plan expansion, but only one quarter of the expansion is in the numbers so far.

### The April 2026 change (confirmed)

- **2 April 2026:** Shopify extended native B2B — company profiles, up to three custom catalogs, volume discounts and quantity rules, vaulted credit cards, payment terms — to **Basic, Grow and Advanced** at no extra cost. Plus keeps unlimited catalogs, direct catalog assignment, partial payments/deposits. Shopify: **"B2B features have consistently been one of the most requested capabilities from non-Plus merchants."** https://www.shopify.com/news/b2b-for-all ; https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/04/03/shopify-adds-b2b-features-dtc-merchants/
- Help Center plan matrix: Companies, Company locations and **Net terms** are ticked for Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/getting-started/plan-features

### Merchant counts

- Shopify (10-K FY2025): "millions of merchants" in 175+ countries; US 44%, EMEA 31%, APAC 16% of revenue. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594805/000159480526000007/shop-20251231.htm
- Third-party live-store count: **3,021,959 live Shopify stores** (US 1,150,327; UK 226,129; CA 145,791; AU 135,714; DE 100,247), StoreLeads, 28 Aug 2026. https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify
- Shopify Plus: **78,442 domains / ~52,294 distinct merchants** (US 37.4%, UK 8.0%, DE 6.8%), StoreLeads. https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/list-of-shopify-plus-stores

**Implication:** before 2 April 2026 the ceiling for native-B2B users was ~52K Plus merchants (B2B a "very small portion" of GMV). After it, ~3.0M live stores — a ~58x increase in *eligible* stores. There is **no published figure** for how many actually sell wholesale or have created a Company. Merchant quote capturing pent-up demand: "It would have been tremendously helpful if there had just been a native B2B functionality on a regular plan." (Justin Dyson, DAX Eyewear, shopify.com/news/b2b-for-all)

**Competitive watch (not fully verified):** two agency write-ups of the Summer '26 Edition (17 June 2026) say net terms with automated invoicing are now native (https://li.solutions/blog/shopify-summer-2026-edition/ ; https://what.digital/shopify-summer-2026-edition-new-updates/). Shopify's own Editions page returned 404. Neither mentions credit limits or checkout blocking.

## 2. Net-terms prevalence, late payment, DSO, bad debt

### Share of B2B trade on credit / demand for terms
- **UK (Atradius, 27 May 2025): 49% of all B2B sales on credit; 51% of B2B invoices overdue; bad debt 7%.** https://group.atradius.com/knowledge-and-research/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-united-kingdom-2025
- **US (Atradius 2025): 43% of credit-based B2B sales overdue; bad debt 5%. Canada: 44% overdue; ~6% bad debt.** https://group.atradius.com/knowledge-and-research/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-in-north-america-2025
- **Western Europe (Atradius 2025): 47% of B2B invoices overdue; 6% written off; typical terms 31–60 days.** https://group.atradius.com/knowledge-and-research/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-western-europe-2025
- Vendor: "43% of B2B transactions rely on trade credit" (Fundbox). https://fundbox.com/resources/guides/trade-credit/
- Vendor (Hokodo 2024 survey of 500 UK+EU buyers): **83% would abandon a purchase if no payment terms were offered at checkout**. https://www.hokodo.co/report-2024-b2b-buyer-expectations
- Vendor (Balance, Oct 2024): merchants saw a "38% average increase in new buyer acquisition" after adding net terms. https://www.getbalance.com/guides-and-reports/the-b2b-payments-report/

A Shopify-specific "% of wholesale merchants offering net terms" figure **does not exist publicly**; the Atradius ~45–50%-of-sales-on-credit range is the best proxy.

### Late payment cost to SMEs
- **US (Xero SBI):** paid **9.0 days late** in Q1 2026, 28.8 days to get paid; "56% of small and midsize businesses were owed money at any given time, with the average past-due amount sitting at $17,500." https://www.xero.com/us/guides/late-payment-small-business/ ; days late by country (Dec 2025 quarter): AU 6.6, UK 8.0, US 7.8, NZ 4.5, CA 9.7. https://blog.xero.com/data-insights/small-business-insights-data-late-payment-results/
- **UK (Small Business Commissioner, 31 Jul 2025):** 28% of businesses affected; **14,000 closures/yr**; ~£11bn/yr cost; £26bn owed at any time, **£17,000 per affected business**; 86 hours/yr chasing. https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/late-payments-research-2/ — **FSB 2025: 54% of small firms paid late in the past three months.**
- **Australia:** Payment Times Reporting Regulator, Jan–Jun 2025: **66.5% of small-business invoices paid on time**; 95th-percentile payment time rose **58 → 64 days**. https://paymenttimes.gov.au/news/media-release-regulators-update-202601 — Xero/Accenture (Sep 2022): 48% of AU small-business invoices paid late, **A$1.1bn/yr** cost. https://www.xero.com/au/media-releases/crunch-cash-flow-report-part-2-au/

## 3. Shopify-specific demand signals

### What Shopify's docs say
- Payment terms: **"After the terms expire, customers can still make payments but the order displays as Overdue in customer accounts."** No credit limit, no ordering restriction. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/checkout-and-orders/payment-terms
- Reminders: up to **five** native reminder emails, "on due date" or up to 30 days *after* due — nothing before due, nothing on credit. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/payments/deferred-payments

## 4. What merchants do today without an app

- **Spreadsheets + Xero/QuickBooks reconciliation** — "final_FINAL2.xlsx"; "took an hour of clicking through orders" (threads 669115, 654028).
- **Manual hold via the company "draft orders for review" toggle** — the only native control (replies in 654028).
- **Draft orders + "send invoice, payment due later"** for merchants without Company setup (https://community.shopify.com/t/setting-up-net30-payment-options-for-wholesale-customers/397619 ; https://community.shopify.com/t/selling-to-business-and-not-collecting-pymt/367483).
- **Hand-rolled Flow reminder loops** with order tagging (threads 572816, 295506, 275668, 373542).
- **Weekly manual review list** (thread 673341).
- **Customer-tag + third-party wholesale app** for pre-April non-Plus stores (https://community.shopify.com/t/b2c-and-b2b-with-net-30-accounts-on-same-website/364841/2).

## 5. Shopify App Store economics

- **Store size:** "more than 21,000 apps"; Shopify **paid developers $1.3B in 2025**. https://www.shopify.com/news/billion-dollar-ecosystem — trackers: 17,591 active apps (Apr 2026, +51% YoY, 564 added in 30 days; ~40% with a free option; average plan $50–60/mo) https://craftberry.co/articles/shopify-app-store-statistics

## TAM / SAM / SOM (assumptions explicit; confidence labelled)

**Anchor facts:** 3.02M live stores; ~52K Plus merchants; B2B Plus-only until 2 Apr 2026 and "a very small portion" of GMV; ~45–50% of B2B sales on credit (Atradius); price $29/mo = **$348/yr**.

**TAM — stores that sell B2B/wholesale on Shopify (confidence: LOW).** Assumption: 3–8% of live stores sell wholesale in any form.
→ 3.02M × 3–8% = **~90K–240K stores** → × $348 = **$31M–$84M ARR** if every one paid.

**SAM — stores on *native* B2B (Companies) that extend net terms (confidence: LOW–MEDIUM).** Native-B2B adoption among wholesale sellers: assume 25% by end-2026. Offer net terms: assume 50%.
→ 90K–240K × 25% × 50% = **~11K–30K stores** → **$3.9M–$10.4M ARR**.
Cross-check via Plus: 52K × 10–15% using B2B = 5–8K pre-April native-B2B merchants; adding non-Plus adopters over 2026–27 reaches the low end.

**SOM — realistic capture (confidence: LOW).** Seven direct competitors launched Apr–Jul 2026 with 0 reviews; the median app earns <$1K/mo; 66% of apps never pass 10 reviews.
- 12-month: **0.5–1% of SAM = 55–300 paying merchants → $19K–$104K ARR** (requires ~1,000–3,000 installs at 8–18% install→paid).
- 36-month, if it becomes the category's reviewed leader: **2–3% of SAM = 220–900 paying → $77K–$313K ARR**.
Material movers: (a) Shopify shipping native credit limits/blocking; (b) whether the app is the first in this cluster to cross ~100 reviews.

**Numbers that do not exist publicly (do not invent):** count of Shopify merchants with a Company record; count offering net terms; B2B GMV in dollars; Shopify-app-specific trial→paid conversion.
