## Assumption-Delta Architecture Checkpoint

**Decision: add-alongside.** Phase 1 keyed everything to Shop (domain string). Phase 2 introduces Company as a second identity axis *under* each shop. No Phase 1 abstraction assumed shop-is-the-only-key in a way that requires promotion: all new models carry a plain `shop` column exactly like ComplianceRequest, redactShop() extends per-table (one deleteMany per new model), and the scheduler contract generalizes from a comment to the structural `forEachActiveShop` helper (CF-3). Recorded in 02-01-PLAN.md frontmatter (`assumption_delta`).
